Step 2: Think, pair, share

You have 3 minutes. Tell your partner which candidate you would hire. Use these sentence starters.
3:00
  • “I would hire this person because…”
  • “I would not hire this person because…”
  • “The most important quality is…”
  • “Even though the candidate…, I think…”
Remember: listen to your partner, then swap. You can change your mind after you hear a good reason.
